ChromaQA: Intelligent Cloud-Based Color Quality Control for Modern Print Production

AdaSphere · Color Quality Control

In today’s highly competitive printing and packaging industry, achieving consistent color quality is no longer optional—it’s essential. Every color variation can lead to wasted materials, increased production costs, customer complaints, and delayed deliveries. ChromaQA is an intuitive cloud-based Color Quality Control (QC) software designed to eliminate these challenges by transforming color measurements into clear, precise, and actionable guidance. Built with simplicity in mind, ChromaQA enables press operators and ink technicians to achieve exceptional color accuracy without requiring advanced expertise in spectrophotometers or complex software. By removing the guesswork from color matching, ChromaQA helps reduce make-ready times, improve production efficiency, and maintain consistent color throughout every print run.

Why Choose ChromaQA?

ChromaQA empowers print operations to detect color issues early—before they become expensive production problems. With intuitive workflows and cloud-based accessibility, the software helps:

  • Reduce press make-ready times
  • Improve color consistency throughout production
  • Identify ink quality issues before they reach the press
  • Minimize waste and rework
  • Enhance productivity and profitability
  • Support standardized color management across multiple locations

To meet the diverse needs of printers, ChromaQA is available in three specialized editions.

ChromaQA SUITE – Complete Enterprise Color Management

ChromaQA SUITE is a comprehensive color quality management solution designed for packaging printers seeking end-to-end control of their color workflow. The software supports:

  • Spot color management
  • Four-color process (CMYK) printing
  • Extended Color Gamut (ECG) workflows
  • G7+ and ISO 12647 compliance tracking

Beyond color measurement, ChromaQA SUITE offers detailed job reporting, data analysis, and customizable scorecards that provide valuable insights for quality managers, production teams, and brand owners. These reporting capabilities help organizations drive continuous process improvement while ensuring consistent print quality across every job.

ChromaQA INK – Centralized Ink Color Management

ChromaQA INK is a cloud-based solution designed to simplify the storage, management, and sharing of color standards across printing organizations of all sizes. By creating a centralized color database, ink technicians can quickly locate existing color standards, determine when new drawdowns are required, and eliminate unnecessary duplication. The software also enables comprehensive quality control testing of newly mixed ink batches, ensuring every ink formulation meets specification before reaching the pressroom. This proactive approach reduces waste, prevents production delays, and improves overall print consistency.

ChromaQA LITE – Simple, Affordable Spot Color Matching

Designed specifically for small and mid-sized packaging printers, ChromaQA LITE delivers powerful spot color matching in an easy-to-use and cost-effective package. Its streamlined interface provides press operators with clear, actionable color guidance and intuitive pass/fail indicators, making accurate color matching faster than ever. By removing uncertainty from the color adjustment process, ChromaQA LITE significantly reduces make-ready times, improves production efficiency, and helps printers maintain consistent color throughout every press run.

The Future of Color Quality Management

Whether you’re managing a single pressroom or multiple production facilities, ChromaQA provides the tools needed to standardize color quality, streamline workflows, and improve operational efficiency. From enterprise-scale quality management with ChromaQA SUITE to centralized ink control with ChromaQA INK and affordable pressroom color matching with ChromaQA LITE, the platform offers a solution for every stage of the print production process. With intelligent cloud-based technology, real-time guidance, and simplified workflows, ChromaQA helps printers produce accurate, repeatable, and consistent color—job after job.
