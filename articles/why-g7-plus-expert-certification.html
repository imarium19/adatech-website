Why G7+™ Expert Certification Is Essential

AdaSphere · G7+

In today’s competitive printing and packaging industry, color consistency is no longer a luxury—it is a necessity. Brands expect their colors to appear identical across different print technologies, substrates, and production locations. To meet this demand, the industry relies on G7+, the globally recognized print calibration and color management methodology that ensures visual similarity and predictable color reproduction across all print processes.

What is G7+?

G7+ is the latest evolution of the renowned G7 methodology, developed to align color appearance across traditional and emerging print technologies. It builds upon the proven principles of G7 while expanding its capabilities to support modern commercial printing, packaging production, and advanced digital print workflows. Whether a company operates offset, flexographic, gravure, screen, wide-format, digital toner, inkjet, or dye-sublimation presses, G7+ provides a measurable and standardized approach to achieving accurate and repeatable color results. By establishing a common visual appearance target, G7+ enables print providers to deliver consistent output regardless of the printing process being used.

The Legacy of G7

Since its introduction in 2005, G7 has transformed the printing industry by simplifying color calibration and process control. The methodology has become the foundation for several widely adopted international print standards and specifications. Over the years, G7 has helped thousands of printing companies improve efficiency, reduce waste, shorten make-ready times, and achieve superior color consistency. Its success has led to widespread adoption across commercial printing, packaging, publishing, and digital printing environments worldwide. G7+ continues this legacy by addressing the evolving needs of modern print production and supporting the increasing demand for cross-platform color alignment.

Why G7+ Matters

Brands often produce marketing materials and packaging using multiple print technologies. Without a standardized color management system, the same design can appear different from one production method to another. G7+ addresses this challenge by providing:

  • Consistent visual appearance across print technologies
  • Predictable and repeatable color reproduction
  • Improved process control and quality assurance
  • Reduced production waste and rework
  • Greater confidence in meeting customer color expectations
  • Alignment with global print and color standards

By implementing G7+, print service providers can enhance productivity while delivering the level of color accuracy demanded by today’s global brands.

Becoming a G7+ Expert

A G7+ Expert is a certified professional who has demonstrated advanced knowledge in color management, process control, and print quality assurance using the G7+ methodology. Certified G7+ Experts are trained to:

  • Implement G7+ calibration and process control systems
  • Manage color workflows across multiple print technologies
  • Analyze color and print quality issues
  • Identify root causes of production inconsistencies
  • Apply corrective actions to bring systems back into compliance
  • Maintain predictable and repeatable print performance

To earn certification, candidates must successfully complete PRINTING United Alliance’s G7+ Expert Training Program and pass a comprehensive certification examination with a minimum proficiency score of 90 percent. The certification remains valid for two years, ensuring that professionals stay current with evolving industry standards and best practices.

Demonstrate Leadership in Print Excellence

As the printing and packaging industry continues to evolve, expertise in color management has become a critical competitive advantage. Organizations that employ certified G7+ Experts gain access to the industry's most advanced methodologies for print calibration, process optimization, and quality control. G7+ certification not only validates technical competence but also demonstrates a commitment to excellence, consistency, and customer satisfaction. For print professionals seeking to advance their careers and for organizations striving to achieve world-class color performance, becoming a G7+ Expert represents a significant step toward operational excellence and industry leadership. Achieve predictable color. Deliver consistent results. Become a G7+ Expert and lead the future of print production.
